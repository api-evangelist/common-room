# Common Room

> The AI-native go-to-market platform for buyer intelligence and action. Identify, prioritize, and act on the right buyers with precision. Common Room unifies first-party customer data with real-world buyer signals into a continuously updated system of buyer intelligence. AI agents to help revenue teams prioritize, understand what’s changing, and execute with precision.

### Native Signals

- [Overview](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/signals/common-room-native-signals/): Common Room provides a set of out-of-the-box signals to ensure you can have a well rounded view of contacts and organizations with the signals that will help you best identify them.
- [Chrome Extension](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/signals/common-room-native-signals/chrome-extension/): Common Room’s LinkedIn extension for the Google Chrome browser allows you to extract valuable signals from websites, starting with LinkedIn. Using this extension, you can easily add contacts, posts, comments, and reactions directly into Common Room from anywhere on LinkedIn.
- [Contact Defaults](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/signals/common-room-native-signals/default-contact-signals/): Common Room provides a default set of signals for Contacts. These signals can range from standard attributes about the Contact such as job title or location but also cover other aspects of Contacts such as whether they have recently changed jobs.
- [Organization Defaults](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/signals/common-room-native-signals/default-organization-signals/): Common Room provides a default set of signals for Organizations. These signals can range from standard attributes about the contact such as industry or location but also cover other aspects of Organizations such job listings and news articles.
- [Job Changes](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/signals/common-room-native-signals/job-changes/): Keeping tabs on key Contacts as they change jobs can be a great way to sell into new Organizations. With Common Room’s job change tracking, you can identify Contacts that have recently changed jobs but also take it one step further by using signals from their prior organization (e.g. their prior organization was a customer) to better identify key opportunities.
- [Job Listings](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/signals/common-room-native-signals/job-listings/): Use current and historical job listings from an organization as a signal to better understand their priorities. Job listings can hold key context about where a company is focused and can hold clues about strategic priorities (e.g. a company is focused on digital transformation), desired skills (e.g. LLM prompt writing), and technologies that they may be investing in (e.g. looking for experience implementing a work management tool).
- [News and Events](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/signals/common-room-native-signals/news-and-events/): Use news articles and current events about an organization as a signal to better understand their priorities. News and events can hold key context about where a company is focused (e.g. increasing operating margins) or what type of events a company may be responding to (e.g. a data security breach).

- [Segment Creation](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/segments/create-a-segment/): This guide will cover cover how to create Contact and Organization Segments.
- [Actions - Tasks](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/segments/segment-actions/segment-tasks/): Segment tasks allow you to view your users as a list of actionable tasks that you can complete or snooze until a later date. Tasks are unique to each Segment, so when you complete or snooze a task, it only relates to Contact or Organizations within that Segment. This doc outlines how to use the task view in your Contact Segments.
- [Actions - Statuses](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/segments/segment-statuses/): This section covers how to track and organize Contact cohorts and programs by applying, editing, and creating statuses in a Segment.
- [Manage - Settings and Log](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/segments/segment-management/settings-and-log/): The settings tab lets you automatically manage Segments, Organizations, and their statuses.
- [Manage - Share and Notify](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/segments/segment-management/sharing-and-notification/): You can share a Segment with team Contacts who are collaborating with you on the Contacts and Organizations in the Segment.
- [Manage - Default Columns](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/segments/segment-default-columns/): Input default columns that will be used in all segments created. Ensure your segments show the most valuable signal each time one is created. If desired, users can still over-ride the defaults but this feature allows you to ensure the starting point matches your preferences.
- [Team Segments](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/segments/team-segments/): Team segments allow users to create a centrally controlled and managed segment that they can assign team members to. When a team member uses the team segment, their view of the contacts and orgs within the segment will be personally tailored to them.

### Contacts

- [Contacts](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/contacts/): Contacts in Common Room reflect real people in the real world —- not just someone who exists in a database. Using Person360™, we can auto-magically enrich and merge signals from any connected signal like social, community, data warehouse, CRM and more into one unified Contacts profile.
- [Profiles](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/contacts/contact-profiles/): A Contact profile is created for every individual Contact who's part of your data sources and integrations. Contact profiles surface details about the Contact, including their role, title, location, contact information, unified engagement & activity across your sources, activity timeline, and content they’ve shared.
- [Teammates](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/contacts/managing-team-members/): This article provides step-by-step instructions for how to add or remove someone as a team member.
- [Contact Tags](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/contacts/contact-tags/): Contact tags allow you to filter for community Contacts with certain attributes, and can be used to filter Contacts on the Contacts, Activity, and Organizations pages.
- [Add or Update](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/contacts/add-contacts/): This article covers how to add individual Contacts and add or update Contacts in bulk.
- [Filter and Sort](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/contacts/filter-and-sort-contacts/): This article is an overview of using filters, filter groups, and sorting. Filters enable you to explore, narrow down, and learn more about your Contacts.  While the Contacts page is the focus of this article, filters can be applied in other areas of Common Room like Segments, Workflows, Activity, and Reporting.
- [Merge Contacts](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/contacts/proposed-merges/): When a new source is integrated into Common Room, we'll automatically work to merge any Contacts that match those already in Common Room using our Person360™ identity resolution engine. However, if we don't have 100% confidence in a match, we'll propose Contacts for you to review and merge. You can also merge any two profiles from the Contacts page.
- [Bulk Messaging](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/contacts/bulk-messaging/): Bulk messaging is a lightweight way to send direct Slack messages to your Contacts from your own personal Slack account. While you are able to select multiple people at a time, each message will be sent as an individual direct message to every Contact you've selected.
- [Impact Points](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/contacts/impact-points/): Impact points help you quickly understand which Contacts are driving the greatest impact within your community. To calculate Impact points, different Contact activities are weighted based on their significance within your community. For example, a LinkedIn comment may have a higher weight for Impact points compared to a LinkedIn reaction.

### Activity

- [Activity overview](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/activity-page/): Activity shows the types of engagement happening across your community - joins, comments, replies, retweets, RSVPs - anything you can monitor within a given signal. Activity is essentially your community newsfeed. It gives you a complete look at who’s saying what and where, whether it’s with other Contacts or to your organization directly.
- [Contact Initiated](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/activity-page/contact-initiated-activity/): Common Room provides precise insights about Contact engagement in your community by differentiating between activities that are Contact initiated and those that are received by Contacts. We make this distinction because activities taken by a Contact are better indicators of intent and provide more actionable data for you to use.
- [Filter, Tag, and Delete](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/activity-page/activity-filters-tags-and-deletion/): Activity filters allow you to surface activity based on different attributes, including signals, Contact tags, activity tags, sentiment, whether the activity is from an internal teammate, and auto-categories such as Bug/issue, Product question, and Content attached.
- [Sentiment](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/activity-page/sentiment/): Common Room surfaces sentiment from activity to help your teams get a pulse on how Contacts are feeling, especially during events which significantly impact the community.
- [Auto-Categories](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/activity-page/auto-categories/): Common Room analyzes activity to best approximate what a Contact is saying, sharing, or feeling. We automatically group together and surface categories of activity that may be most relevant for your team through the following preset categories: Product complaint, feature request, product appreciation, bug / issue, product question, content attached, and account support. This way you know exactly what follow-up actions to take based on the activity category. In the case of conversations, we’ll even tag multiple categories when applicable, to provide you with an idea of where the conversation may be heading.
- [Detecting Viral Content](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/activity-page/detecting-viral-content/): Common Room enables you to monitor and alert on activities that have gone "viral" in your community. We currently support detecting viral content across Reddit posts and Dev.to articles. You can filter for activities that have gone viral and also set up team alerts and workflows to fire when viral content is detected.

### Organizations

- [Profiles](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/organizations-page/organization-profiles/): Each organization has an organization profile page. You can customize what information is surfaced at the top of every organization profile page in your Common Room with key fields, such as revenue, opportunity stage, account owner, or location.
- [Tags](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/organizations-page/organization-tags/): Organization tags allow you to filter for organizations with certain attributes, along with the Contacts who work at those organizations. Organization tags can be used to filter on the Contacts, Activity, and Organizations pages.
- [Filters and Sorting](https://www.commonroom.io/docs/using-common-room/organizations-page/filters-and-sorting/): Filters on the Organizations page enable you to explore, narrow down, and learn more about your organizations and their Contacts. You can filter the list of organizations by organization filters (shown below), as well as Contact, activity, and signal-specific filters. You can apply one or more filters at a time using AND, OR, and NOT operators to help identify specific organizations based on certain attributes.
