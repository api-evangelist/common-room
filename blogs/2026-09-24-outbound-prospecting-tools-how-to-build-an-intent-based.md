Outbound prospecting in 2026 isn’t about bigger contact lists—it’s about building a connected, signal-based stack that helps reps spot in-market buyers, resolve them to the right person, and act fast. This guide breaks down the five core tool categories and explains why signal quality, prioritization, and integration matter more than raw database size.
