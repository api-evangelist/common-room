Choosing a data enrichment company in 2026 is no longer about buying more records — it’s about giving your CRM and AI systems trustworthy, deduplicated buyer data they can actually act on. This guide breaks down the eight criteria that matter most, from coverage and match rates to identity resolution and AI-readiness, so you can shortlist vendors that improve your stack instead of adding more cleanup work.
