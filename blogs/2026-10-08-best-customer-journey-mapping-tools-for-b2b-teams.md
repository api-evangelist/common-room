Most B2B journey maps go stale fast because they miss the messy, multi-person buying behavior happening across AI search, communities, review sites, and your product. This guide compares the top journey mapping tool categories for 2026—and shows how to pair static maps with live buyer signals so marketing and sales can act on real account journeys.
